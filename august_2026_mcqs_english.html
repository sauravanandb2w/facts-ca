Important Days & Events

8 Questions
Q1
On which date was Article 370 abrogated, granting the government authority to bifurcate J&K?
(A) 4th August 2019
✅ (B) 5th August 2019
(C) 15th August 2019
(D) 26th August 2019
📖 Answer: (B)
Article 370 was abrogated on 5th August 2019. The Jammu & Kashmir Reorganisation Act, 2019 bifurcated the state into two Union Territories: J&K (with legislature) and Ladakh (without legislature).
Q2
Which day is observed as Hiroshima Day, commemorating the atomic bombing during World War II?
✅ (A) 6th August
(B) 7th August
(C) 8th August
(D) 9th August
📖 Answer: (A)
Hiroshima was bombed on 6th August 1945. Nagasaki was bombed on 9th August 1945. Both dates are observed internationally to promote nuclear disarmament.
Q3
World Elephant Day and International Youth Day are both observed on which date?
(A) 10th August
(B) 11th August
✅ (C) 12th August
(D) 13th August
📖 Answer: (C)
Both World Elephant Day and International Youth Day are observed on 12th August every year. World Elephant Day was first observed in 2012.
Q4
National Sports Day in India is observed on 29th August. Why is this date significant?

Economy & Finance (RBI Policy)

7 Questions
Q9
What is the current Repo Rate as decided in the August 2026 RBI MPC meeting?
(A) 5.00%
✅ (B) 5.25%
(C) 5.50%
(D) 6.00%
📖 Answer: (B)
The RBI Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) kept the Repo Rate unchanged at 5.25% in August 2026. The Repo Rate is the rate at which RBI lends money to commercial banks.
Q10
What is the Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) rate after the August 2026 RBI policy?
(A) 4.75%
✅ (B) 5.00%
(C) 5.25%
(D) 5.50%
📖 Answer: (B)
The SDF rate is 5.00% (Repo Rate minus 25 bps). SDF allows banks to park excess liquidity with RBI without any collateral. It replaced the reverse repo as the floor rate.
Q11
What is the Bank Rate (also the MSF rate) after the August 2026 RBI MPC decision?
(A) 5.00%
(B) 5.25%
✅ (C) 5.50%
(D) 5.75%
📖 Answer: (C)
The Bank Rate / Marginal Standing Facility (MSF) rate is 5.50% (Repo Rate plus 25 bps). Banks can borrow overnight from RBI at the MSF rate against approved government securities.
Q12
RBI revised India's GDP growth forecast for FY2026-27 to which figure in August 2026?
(A) 6.5%
(B) 6.6%
✅ (C) 6.7%
(D) 7.0%
📖 Answer: (C)
RBI revised GDP growth forecast upward to 6.7% from the earlier estimate of 6.6%, reflecting improving economic conditions and consumption recovery.
Q13
RBI's CPI inflation projection for FY2026-27 stands at which figure?
(A) 4.5%
(B) 4.8%
✅ (C) 5.0%
(D) 5.2%
📖 Answer: (C)
RBI projected CPI inflation at 5.0% for FY2026-27. CPI (Consumer Price Index) tracks changes in the price level of a basket of consumer goods and services.
Q14
What does SDF stand for in the context of RBI's monetary policy tools?
(A) Special Deposit Framework
✅ (B) Standing Deposit Facility
(C) Statutory Discount Fund
(D) Structured Deposit Formula
📖 Answer: (B)
SDF stands for Standing Deposit Facility. Introduced in April 2022, it is a liquidity absorption tool that allows RBI to absorb surplus liquidity from banks without providing collateral. The SDF rate is the floor of the LAF (Liquidity Adjustment Facility) corridor.
Q15
The LAF corridor in India's monetary policy consists of which pair of rates?
(A) Repo Rate and Bank Rate
✅ (B) SDF Rate and MSF Rate
(C) CRR and SLR
(D) Repo Rate and Reverse Repo Rate
📖 Answer: (B)
The Liquidity Adjustment Facility (LAF) corridor is bounded by the SDF rate (floor) at 5.00% and the MSF rate (ceiling) at 5.50%, with the Repo Rate at 5.25% as the policy rate in the middle.

(B) 4 years
✅ (C) 5 years

The tenure fixed for tribunal Chairpersons and Members is 5 years under the new bill. Earlier, there were issues with short and uncertain tenures affecting tribunal independence.

(A) 65 years

IST Rules 2026

5 Questions
Q50
Under the Legal Metrology IST Rules 2026, Indian Standard Time (IST) will be maintained and calculated by which two institutions?
✅ (A) NPL (CSIR) and ISRO
(B) IIT Delhi and DRDO
(C) BIS and RBI
(D) IMD and ISRO
📖 Answer: (A)
IST will be calculated using atomic clocks at CSIR-NPL (National Physical Laboratory) and ISRO's NavIC (India's own GPS system), ensuring precision to microseconds.
Q51
Under the Legal Metrology IST Rules 2026, how many days are given as a transition period after the official gazette publication?
(A) 90 days
(B) 120 days
(C) 150 days
✅ (D) 180 days
📖 Answer: (D)
A 180-day transition period is given after the official gazette publication, allowing all institutions to align their timekeeping systems with the new IST standard.
Q52
IST (Indian Standard Time) corresponds to GMT + how many hours?
(A) +5:00
✅ (B) +5:30
(C) +5:45
(D) +6:00
📖 Answer: (B)
IST is GMT+5:30, based on the 82°30'E meridian passing through Mirzapur (Prayagraj district), Uttar Pradesh. India does not observe daylight saving time.
Q53
Which Ministry is the nodal ministry for implementation of the Legal Metrology IST Rules 2026?
(A) Ministry of Science and Technology
(B) Ministry of Electronics and IT
✅ (C) Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution
(D) Ministry of Finance
📖 Answer: (C)
The Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution is the nodal ministry for Legal Metrology matters including the IST Rules 2026.
Q54
India's NavIC system, which helps calculate IST, is India's own equivalent of which global system?
(A) WiFi
✅ (B) GPS
(C) Bluetooth
(D) Satellite TV
📖 Answer: (B)
NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) is India's own regional navigation satellite system, equivalent to GPS (USA), GLONASS (Russia), Galileo (EU), or BeiDou (China). It is operated by ISRO.
