A. Birmingham, England
B. Glasgow, Scotland
C. Ahmedabad, India

CWG 2026 were held in Glasgow, Scotland from July 23 – August 2, 2026. Memory: 'Glass glow kiya Scotland ne' → Glasgow, Scotland.

B. Finnie – (Scottish creature)

3

A. 2nd
B. 3rd
C. 4th
D. 5th

India's CWG 2026 tally: 13 Gold, 17 Silver, 9 Bronze = 39 total. Key number: 9 Bronze medals.

CWG 2026 – Key Indian Athlete Milestones (7 MCQs)
13
Mirabai Chanu won her ___ consecutive Gold medal at CWG 2026 in the Women's 48kg weightlifting event.
A. 1st
B. 2nd
C. 3rd
D. 4th
Mirabai Chanu's CWG 2026 gold was her 3rd CONSECUTIVE gold: 2018 Gold Coast + 2022 Birmingham + 2026 Glasgow. She also won Silver in 2014 Glasgow (her first CWG medal). Total: 4th CWG medal, 3rd consecutive gold.
14
Sharmila Dhankar created history at CWG 2026 by becoming the first Indian to win gold in Para Athletics. In which event?
A. 100m T47
B. Discus Throw F44
C. Women's Shotput F57
D. Long Jump T64
Sharmila Dhankar won Gold in Women's Shotput F57 (Para Athletics) with a throw of 9.81 metres — the first Indian to win gold in Para Athletics at CWG. Memory: 'Shotput gira, DHAN karke aawaaz aayi → Dhankar'.
15

A. Murali Sreeshankar
B. Gulveer Singh

D. Tejasvi Shankar
Sarvesh Kushare won India's first-ever Silver in Men's High Jump (2.25m clearance). Memory: 'Sarv = sab, ek dum oocha kood gaya Kushare' → High Jump.
16

CWG – Static Knowledge (10 MCQs)
28
When and where were the FIRST Commonwealth Games held, and what were they called then?
A. 1934, London – British Empire Games
B. 1930, Hamilton Canada – British Empire Games
C. 1938, Sydney – Commonwealth Games
D. 1926, London – Imperial Games
First Commonwealth Games: 1930, Hamilton, Canada. Name then: 'British Empire Games'. India had not yet participated (joined in 1934). Memory: '1930 = start, Hamilton Canada, British Empire Games'.
29
In which year was the name 'Commonwealth Games' (current name) officially adopted?
A. 1954
B. 1966
C. 1978
D. 1982
Name evolution: British Empire Games (1930) → British Empire & Commonwealth Games (1954) → British Commonwealth Games → Commonwealth Games (1978). Memory: '1978 = current name Commonwealth Games'.
30
India FIRST participated in the Commonwealth Games in which year?
A. 1930
B. 1934
C. 1938
D. 1946
India first participated in CWG in 1934 (Second edition) in London, UK. India's first CWG medal was also won in 1934 by wrestler Rashid Anwar (Bronze, Men's 74kg freestyle).
31
Who won India's FIRST Commonwealth Games medal and in which sport?
A. Milkha Singh – Athletics
B. PT Usha – Athletics
C. Rashid Anwar – Wrestling
D. Dara Singh – Wrestling
India's first CWG medal: Rashid Anwar (Wrestler), Bronze, Men's 74kg Freestyle Wrestling, 1934 London. Memory: 'Rashid = Rash mein daud ke aakar Wrestling mein medal jeeta'.
32
Who won India's FIRST GOLD medal at the Commonwealth Games and in which city?
A. Rashid Anwar – London 1934
B. Milkha Singh – Cardiff, Wales 1958
C. PT Usha – Brisbane 1982
D. Geet Sethi – Kuala Lumpur 1998
Milkha Singh (the Flying Sikh) won India's first CWG Gold in 1958 at Cardiff, Wales. The movie 'Bhaag Milkha Bhaag' is based on him. He was a sprinter (running event). Memory: 'Milkha Cardiff mein run karke Gold laaya'.
33
In which year did India host the Commonwealth Games, and what was India's best-ever performance at those Games?
A. 1982 Delhi – 75 medals
B. 2010 Delhi – 101 medals
C. 2006 Delhi – 88 medals
D. 2018 Delhi – 90 medals
India hosted CWG in 2010 in Delhi, and won 101 medals — India's best-ever CWG performance. Memory: '2010 Delhi = India ka best, 101 medals'.
34
The Commonwealth Games are held every ___ years. In which years were they NOT held due to World Wars?
A. 2 years – 1940 and 1944
B. 4 years – 1942 and 1946
C. 4 years – 1940 and 1944
D. 6 years – 1942 and 1946
CWG are held every 4 years. They were NOT held in 1942 and 1946 due to World War II. All other editions since 1930 have happened regularly.
35
In which year did the Commonwealth Games introduce Para (disability) sports categories for inclusivity?
A. 1986
B. 1994
C. 2002
D. 2006
Para sports categories were introduced in the Commonwealth Games in 2002, to include athletes with disabilities alongside able-bodied athletes.
36
Which THREE countries have historically topped the ALL-TIME Commonwealth Games medal tally?
A. India, Australia, England
B. Australia, England, Canada
C. England, Canada, South Africa
D. Australia, New Zealand, England
All-time CWG medal tally leaders: 1st Australia, 2nd England, 3rd Canada — same order as CWG 2026. India is 4th both in 2026 and all-time. Memory: 'AEC → Australia, England, Canada'.
37
The Commonwealth Games include which nations?
A. All UN member states
B. Former British colonies and Commonwealth member nations
C. Only European nations
D. BRICS nations
Commonwealth Games include nations that were formerly part of the British Empire / Commonwealth of Nations. 'Common' refers to the British Crown as the common link. Also called 'Friendly Games'.
