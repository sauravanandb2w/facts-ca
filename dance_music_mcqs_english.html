Indian Music Foundations (10 MCQs)
1
Which is the oldest text on Indian music, written by Bharat Muni?
A. Brihaddeshi
B. Sangeet Ratnakar
C. Natyashastra
D. Sangeet Sudhakar
Natyashastra by Bharat Muni is the oldest text — it covers drama, dance & music, and is also called the 5th Veda. Memory trick: 'Bharat Muni ki Natyashastra' — Bharat wrote about Natya (drama/dance/music).
2
Brihaddeshi by Matanga Muni was the first text to classify music into Margi and Desi. It also introduced which key concept?
A. Thaats
B. Shruti
C. Raga
D. Bandish
Brihaddeshi (by Matanga Muni) first classified Margi (classical, Vedic, rule-bound) vs Desi (folk, regional) music, and was the first to give the concept of Raga.
3
Which 13th-century text by Sharangdeva is considered the earliest medieval Indian music treatise?
A. Sangeet Sudhakar
B. Sangeet Ratnakar
C. Brihaddeshi
D. Kramik Pustak Malika
Sangeet Ratnakar by Sharangdeva (13th century) is the earliest medieval music treatise. Sangeet Sudhakar by Haripal first classified Hindustani vs Carnatic music.
4
Which text first classified music into Hindustani and Carnatic traditions?
A. Sangeet Ratnakar
B. Natyashastra
C. Sangeet Sudhakar
D. Brihaddeshi
Sangeet Sudhakar by Haripal was the first to classify Hindustani vs Carnatic music.
5
Indian classical music is heptatonic. How many swaras (notes) are there in total including vikrit (modified) swaras?
A. 7
B. 10
C. 12
D. 14
7 shuddha (pure) + 5 vikrit = 12 total. The 5 vikrit: 1 teevra (sharp) = Ma, and 4 komal (flat) = Re, Ga, Dha, Ni. Memory: 'मां तीव्र है' (Ma is teevra).
6
Which swaras are called Achala (fixed/immovable) in Indian classical music?
A. Sa and Ma
B. Sa and Pa
C. Re and Ga
D. Dha and Ni
Sa (Shadja) and Pa (Panchama) are Achala swaras — they never change. All other swaras can be komal or teevra.
7
The concept of 10 Thaats in Hindustani music was introduced by which musicologist?
A. Matanga Muni
B. Bharat Muni
C. Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande
D. Sharangdeva
Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande introduced the 10-Thaat system in his book 'Kramik Pustak Malika'. The 10 thaats: Bilawal, Kalyan, Khamaj, Kafi, Asavari, Bhairav, Bhairavi, Todi, Purvi, Marwa. Thaats are scales — they are NOT sung.
8
Which primary raga is associated with the rainy season at sunset?
A. Bhairav
B. Malkaus
C. Deepak
D. Megh
The 6 primary ragas and their times: Bhairav (dawn), Hindol (spring morning), Deepak (early evening), Sri (near sunset), Malkaus (after midnight), Megh (rainy season sunset).
9
A raga with 5 notes (pentatonic) belongs to which Jaati classification?
A. Sampurna
B. Shadav
C. Audav
D. Poorva
Jaati by number of notes: Audav = 5 notes (pentatonic), Shadav = 6 notes (hexatonic), Sampurna = 7 notes (heptatonic).
10
The smallest microtonal interval that the human ear can identify in Indian music is called?
A. Shruti
B. Saptak
C. Gamak
D. Bandish
Shruti = smallest microtonal interval. There are 22 shrutis in Indian classical music. Saptak = octave of 7 notes. Gamak = ornamental movement of a note.
Taal (Rhythm) (6 MCQs)
11
Teentaal, known as the 'King of Taals', has how many beats?
A. 12
B. 14
C. 16
D. 18
Teentaal = 16 beats, divided as 4+4+4+4. It has 3 taalis (claps) and khali (empty beat) starts at the 9th beat. Most popular taal in Hindustani classical music.
12
Which taal has 12 beats divided into six equal sets of 2?
A. Jhaptaal
B. Ektaal
C. Rupak
D. Kaharwa
Ektaal = 12 beats in pattern 2+2+2+2+2+2 (six sets of 2).
13
Rupak taal is unique because it starts with which beat type?
A. Sam (first beat)
B. Taali (clap)
C. Khali (empty beat)
D. Vibhag
Rupak taal (7 beats: 3+2+2) is the only taal that starts with Khali (empty beat), making it unique. All other taals start with Sam.
14
Which taal with 6 beats (3+3) is commonly used in Thumri and Ghazal?
A. Kaharwa
B. Jhaptaal
C. Dadra
D. Ektaal
Dadra = 6 beats (3+3), used in thumri, ghazal and semi-classical music. Kaharwa = 8 beats (4+4), used in bhajans, folk and film songs.
15
Adi Taal (8 beats, 4+2+2) is the most commonly used taal in which system?
A. Hindustani
B. Carnatic
C. Folk
D. Fusion
Adi Taal (8 beats, 4+2+2) is the most common taal in Carnatic music. Carnatic taal units: Anudhrutam (1 beat), Dhrutam (2 beats), Laghu (variable).
16
In Carnatic music, the Laghu jaati with 7 beats is called?
A. Tisra
B. Khanda
C. Misra
D. Sankeerna
Carnatic Laghu Jaatis by beats: Tisra (3), Chatusra (4), Khanda (5), Misra (7), Sankeerna (9). Memory: Think of 3-4-5-7-9.
Styles of Hindustani Music (6 MCQs)
17
Dhrupad is the oldest surviving form of North Indian classical music. It is traditionally sung in which language?
A. Sanskrit
B. Urdu
C. Braj Bhasha
D. Avadhi
Dhrupad is the oldest surviving Hindustani classical form, associated with Tansen. Sung in Braj Bhasha with tanpura and pakhawaj. It has a spiritual, sober nature with strong focus on rhythm (taal).
18
Which musical form was created by Amir Khusrau and uses meaningless syllables like 'tana dena'?
A. Khayal
B. Tappa
C. Tarana
D. Thumri
Tarana was created by Amir Khusrau. It uses meaningless syllables (tana dena, yala li) and is fast-paced, showcasing vocal agility.
19
Khayal, the most popular Hindustani classical form today, was originated by?
A. Tansen
B. Hussain Shah Sharqi
C. Amir Khusrau
D. Shori Mian
Khayal was originated by Hussain Shah Sharqi. It is free, expressive, and improvisational — very different from the structured Dhrupad.
20
Thumri, the semi-classical form, originated in the court of which ruler?
A. Akbar
B. Bahadur Shah Zafar
C. Nawab Wajid Ali Shah of Awadh
D. Aurangzeb
Thumri originated in the court of Nawab Wajid Ali Shah of Awadh (1847-56) in Lucknow. It is emotional, devotional, and associated with Kathak dance. Roots in Lucknow and Banaras.
21
Which musical form is associated with Punjab and Sindhi folk traditions and was started by Shori Mian?
A. Ghazal
B. Tappa
C. Tarana
D. Dadra
Tappa is a Punjabi and Sindhi folk form started by Shori Mian. It has fast-paced murki (quick ornaments) and a folk-classical blend.
22
Which statement BEST describes Dhrupad compared to Khayal?
A. Dhrupad is free and improvisational; Khayal is structured
B. Dhrupad has a sober, structured nature with emphasis on rhythm; Khayal is lighter and expressive
C. Dhrupad uses thumri; Khayal uses tarana
D. Both are identical in style
Dhrupad = sober atmosphere, structured, strong focus on notes and rhythm (taal), traditionally male singers, spiritual. Khayal = lighter, expressive, improvisational, popular today.
Carnatic Music (4 MCQs)
23
Who is called the 'Pitamah' (grandfather/father figure) of Carnatic music?
A. Tyagaraja
B. Syama Shastri
C. Purandar Das
D. Muthuswami Dikshitar
Purandar Das is called the Pitamah (father) of Carnatic music. The Musical Trinity of Carnatic: Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Shastri — all from Tiruvarur.
24
How many Melakarta ragas form the foundational scale system of Carnatic music?
A. 48
B. 60
C. 72
D. 84
There are 72 Melakarta ragas in the Carnatic system. Swaravali in Carnatic = 7-note scale = Saptak in Hindustani.
25
What is the correct sequence of a Carnatic music performance?
A. Kriti → Varnam → Tillana
B. Varnam → Tillana → Kriti
C. Varnam → Kriti → Tillana
D. Tillana → Varnam → Kriti
Carnatic performance: Varnam (intro/warm-up) → Kriti (main body: Pallavi + Anupallavi + Charanam) → Tillana (fast-paced finale). Tillana in Carnatic = Tarana in Hindustani.
26
Which of the following is a key difference between Hindustani and Carnatic music?
A. Carnatic has 10 thaats; Hindustani has 72
B. Hindustani has the Gharana system; Carnatic does not
C. Both follow the same taal system
D. Carnatic uses Khayal; Hindustani uses Kriti
Hindustani music has the Gharana (lineage/school) system. Carnatic music does NOT have a Gharana system. Hindustani has 10 Thaats; Carnatic has 72 Melakarta ragas.

Gwalior Gharana is the oldest Khayal singing Gharana, founded by Nathan Pir Bakhsh. Pioneer: Vishnu Digambar Paluskar.
28
Pandit Bhimsen Joshi and Gangubai Hangal belong to which vocal Gharana?
A. Gwalior Gharana
B. Kirana Gharana
C. Agra Gharana
D. Patiala Gharana
Kirana Gharana is known for slow meditative alap. Key figure: Ustad Abdul Karim Khan. Artists: Bhimsen Joshi, Gangubai Hangal, Begum Akhtar, Prabha Atre. Founded from Kirana village near Ambala.
29
Which Gharana is known for Dhrupad-Dhamar gayaki (style) and powerful, full-throated singing?
A. Kirana Gharana
B. Rampur-Sahaswan Gharana

D. Mewati Gharana
Agra Gharana: founded by Haji Sujan Khan (son-in-law of Tansen), popularized by Ghagge Khuda Bakhsh. Known for Dhrupad-Dhamar gayaki and powerful voice. Key artist: Faiyaz Khan.
30
Sonu Nigam, Hariharan, and Shaan belong to which classical Gharana? Memory trick: 'Ram Ji Ki Inayat'
A. Kirana Gharana
B. Gwalior Gharana
C. Rampur-Sahaswan Gharana
D. Bhendi Bazaar Gharana
Rampur-Sahaswan Gharana: founded by Inayat Hussain Khan, related to Gwalior tradition. Memory: 'राम जी की इनायत' = Rampur-Sahaswan. Artists: Ghulam Mustafa Khan, Sonu Nigam, Hariharan, Shaan.
31
Girija Devi, known as 'Queen of Thumri', belongs to which Gharana?
A. Lucknow Gharana
B. Kirana Gharana
C. Banaras Gharana
D. Mewati Gharana
Banaras Gharana specializes in Thumri (Purabi ang). It is known for freedom and creativity. Artists: Girija Devi (Queen of Thumri), Siddheshwari Devi, Rasoolan Bai, Rajan-Sajan Mishra.
32
Pandit Jasraj is associated with which Gharana?
A. Jaipur-Atrauli
B. Bhendi Bazaar
C. Mewati
D. Patiala
Mewati Gharana: founded by Ghagge Nazir Khan and Wahid Khan (Jodhpur). Known for khayal with devotional emphasis. Key artist: Pandit Jasraj.
33
Bhendi Bazaar Gharana is known for blending which tradition into Hindustani music?
A. Persian/Sufi elements
B. Carnatic elements
C. Folk music elements
D. Bhakti poetry
Bhendi Bazaar Gharana, founded by three brothers (Chhajju Khan, Nazir Khan, Khadim Hussain Khan), uniquely blended Carnatic elements into Hindustani music. Artists: Lata Mangeshkar, Manna Dey.
34
Bade Ghulam Ali Khan and Parween Sultana belong to which Gharana known for Khayal, Thumri and Ghazal?
A. Kirana
B. Gwalior
C. Agra
D. Patiala
Patiala Gharana: founded by Ustad Fateh Ali Khan and Ustad Ali Bakhsh. Known for khayal/thumri/ghazal and layakari/boltaan. Artists: Bade Ghulam Ali Khan, Parween Sultana, Wadali Brothers, Gulam Ali, Shafqat Amanat Ali.
Musical Instruments & Famous Musicians (13 MCQs)
35
Which instrument is played by Ustad Amjad Ali Khan?
A. Sitar
B. Sarangi

Sarod: Ustad Amjad Ali Khan (most famous), Ali Akbar Khan (Maihar Gharana). Sitar: Pandit Ravi Shankar (Maihar Gharana), Ustad Vilayat Khan (Imdadkhani Gharana).
36
Pandit Shivkumar Sharma is famous for playing which instrument?
A. Sarangi
B. Santoor
C. Sarod
D. Rudra Veena
Santoor: Pandit Shivkumar Sharma. Memory: 'शिवजी को संतूर से नहलाओ' — Shivkumar = Santoor. Santoor is a hammered dulcimer-style instrument.
37
Asad Ali Khan is known for playing which instrument?
A. Sitar
B. Sarangi
C. Bansuri
D. Rudra Veena
Rudra Veena (a variant of Veena): Asad Ali Khan. Memory: 'Rudra roop le liya, I am SAD (Asad)' — Rudra Veena = Asad Ali Khan.
38
Pandit Ram Narayan plays which instrument and belongs to which Gharana?
A. Sitar – Maihar
B. Sarod – Imdadkhani
C. Sarangi – Senia Gharana
D. Santoor – Shahjahanpur
Sarangi: Pandit Ram Narayan, Senia Gharana. Sarangi is a bowed string instrument (Chordophone/Tata Vadya).
39
Vikku Vinayakram (T H Vinayakram) is associated with which instrument?
A. Tabla
B. Mridangam
C. Ghatam
D. Pakhawaj
Ghatam (clay pot percussion): Vikku Vinayakram. Memory: 'Vinayak puja ho rahi hai aur aap Ghatam baja rahe ho'. Ghatam is an Idiophone AND Percussion instrument.
40
Palghat Mani Iyer is associated with which Carnatic percussion instrument?
A. Tabla
B. Ghatam
C. Mridangam
D. Pakhawaj
Mridangam: Palghat Mani Iyer. Mridangam is a membranophone (Avanaddha Vadya) used in Carnatic music — elongated drum played while seated.
41
Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia and Pannalal Ghosh are famous for playing?
A. Shehnai
B. Sarangi
C. Bansuri (Flute)
D. Nadaswaram
Bansuri (Flute): Pandit Hariprasad Chaurasia (Aerophone/Sushira Vadya). Memory: 'Hari hari bansuri'. Pannalal Ghosh: 'panne mein rakhte ho bansuri'.
42
Tota Ram Sharma and Pandit Kaddu Singh are famous for playing which instrument?
A. Tabla
B. Sarangi
C. Pakhawaj
D. Mridangam
Pakhawaj: Tota Ram Sharma, Pandit Kaddu Singh. Memory: 'Pankha (fan) pe tota baitha hai' → Pakhawaj (sounds like Pankha) = Tota Ram Sharma.
43
Which instrument classification (Vadya) includes manjira, ghatam, and jalatarang?
A. Avanaddha Vadya (Membranophone)
B. Ghana Vadya (Idiophone)
C. Sushira Vadya (Aerophone)
D. Tata Vadya (Chordophone)
Ghana Vadya (Idiophone): sound produced by the solid instrument vibrating — no tuning required. Examples: manjira, ghatam, jalatarang, khartal. Key: they are SOLID and NON-TUNABLE.
44
Tabla, mridangam, and dhol are classified under which category?
A. Ghana Vadya (Idiophone)
B. Tata Vadya (Chordophone)
C. Avanaddha Vadya (Membranophone)
D. Sushira Vadya (Aerophone)
Avanaddha Vadya (Membranophone): stretched membrane is struck. Examples: tabla, mridangam, dhol, dholak, congo. Both idiophone AND membranophone fall under the broader Percussion category.
45
Ghatam is correctly classified as which type of instrument? (-asked question)
A. Membranophone only
B. Idiophone only
C. Aerophone
D. Both Membranophone and Idiophone
Ghatam = Idiophone (Ghana Vadya) — it is a solid clay pot, non-tunable. It is ALSO a Percussion instrument (broader category). If both Idiophone and Percussion are options, choose Idiophone as it is more specific and accurate.
46
Which gharana specializes in Tabla and includes Alla Rakha Khan and Zakir Hussain?
A. Delhi Gharana
B. Banaras Gharana
C. Punjab Gharana
D. Farukhabad Gharana
Punjab Gharana (Tabla): Alla Rakha Khan (guru), Zakir Hussain (shishya). Delhi Gharana is the oldest tabla Gharana (Ghamu Khan, Inam Ali Khan). Banaras Gharana Tabla: Kishan Maharaj.
47
L Subramaniam and N Rajam are associated with which instrument in Carnatic music?
A. Sitar
B. Sarangi
C. Violin
D. Venu (Flute)
Violin: L Subramaniam (Carnatic violin maestro), N Rajam. The violin was adopted into Carnatic music and these artists are its most famous exponents.
Classical Dance – Foundations (5 MCQs)
48
Natyashastra by Bharat Muni is also called the 5th Veda. It is the earliest written source on?
A. Only music theory
B. Only rhythm patterns
C. Classical dance, drama, and music
D. Temple architecture
Natyashastra by Bharat Muni covers drama, dance, and music. It is called the 5th Veda (after Rig, Yajur, Sama, Atharva Vedas). It is the earliest written text on classical dance.
49
In classical dance theory, Tandava represents masculine energy and Lasya represents feminine grace. Who are these associated with?
A. Brahma and Saraswati
B. Shiva and Shakti
C. Vishnu and Lakshmi
D. Ram and Sita
Tandava = masculine, bold, powerful (Shiva's dance). Lasya = feminine, graceful, lyrical (Shakti's dance). Both elements are present in Indian classical dances.
50
The three components of Indian classical dance are Nritta, Nritya, and Natya. Which is the 'expressive' component?
A. Nritta (pure dance movements)
B. Nritya (expressive/emotional dance)
C. Natya (dance drama)
D. All three are identical
Nritta = pure dance (movements only). Nritya = expressive dance (facial expressions, emotions — communicating through abhinaya). Natya = dance drama (narrating a story). All three combine to form complete classical dance.
51
Sangeet Natak Akademi (est. 1953) recognizes how many classical dance forms?
A. 6
B. 8
C. 9
D. 10
Sangeet Natak Akademi (India's first national academy for performing arts) recognizes 8 classical dance forms: Bharatanatyam, Kuchipudi, Kathakali, Mohini Attam, Odissi, Manipuri, Kathak, Sattriya.
52
Which dance form is recognized as the 9th classical dance by the Ministry of Culture but NOT by Sangeet Natak Akademi?
A. Yakshagana
B. Chhau
C. Bhagavata Mela
D. Theyyam
Chhau is recognized as the 9th classical dance by the Ministry of Culture. Sangeet Natak Akademi does not include it because it has folk elements (martial, tribal, folk traditions). It is semi-classical in nature.
Kathak (7 MCQs)
53
Kathak is the ONLY Indian classical dance form with significant influence from which tradition?
A. Buddhist
B. Jain
C. Islamic (Mughal)
D. Tribal
Kathak is the only Indian classical dance with Islamic/Mughal influence. It transitioned from temples (devotional) to Mughal courts (entertainment). This court transition gave it the Mughal touch — ghazal, thumri, layakari.
54
The 'Golden Age' of Kathak was during the reign of?
A. Emperor Akbar
B. Nawab Wajid Ali Shah of Awadh
C. Tipu Sultan
D. Maharaja Ranjit Singh
The Golden Age of Kathak was during Nawab Wajid Ali Shah's reign (Lucknow/Awadh, 1847-56). This same period also saw Thumri flourish. He founded the Lucknow Gharana of Kathak.
55
Who founded the Jaipur Gharana of Kathak?
A. Wajid Ali Shah
B. Bhanuji
C. Janaki Prasad
D. Chakradhar Singh
Kathak Gharanas and founders: Lucknow Gharana → Wajid Ali Shah | Jaipur Gharana → Bhanuji | Raigarh Gharana → Chakradhar Singh | Banaras Gharana → Janaki Prasad. Memory: 'Jaipur ka Bhanu (sun)'.
56
Banaras Gharana of Kathak was founded by?
A. Lachhu Maharaj
B. Birju Maharaj
C. Janaki Prasad
D. Chakradhar Singh
Banaras Gharana → Janaki Prasad. Memory: 'Banaras ka paan = prasad → Janaki Prasad'. Birju Maharaj belongs to Lucknow (Kalka-Bindadin) Gharana.
57
Birju Maharaj, the legendary Kathak dancer, belongs to which Gharana?
A. Jaipur Gharana
B. Banaras Gharana
C. Raigarh Gharana
D. Lucknow (Kalka-Bindadin) Gharana
Birju Maharaj belongs to Lucknow Gharana (specifically Kalka-Bindadin Gharana). He learned from Lachhu Maharaj (his uncle). Lachhu Maharaj choreographed for films like Mughal-e-Azam and Pakeezah.
58
Who is called the 'Empress of Kathak'?
A. Kumudini Lakhia
B. Shobhana Narayanan
C. Sitara Devi
D. Lady Leela Sokhey
Sitara Devi = Empress of Kathak. She belonged to the Banaras Gharana. Lady Leela Sokhey is credited with reviving Kathak in the 20th century. Kumudini Lakhia modernized Kathak.
59
Raigarh Gharana of Kathak was founded by?
A. Janaki Prasad
B. Bhanuji
C. Chakradhar Singh
D. Wajid Ali Shah
Raigarh Gharana → Chakradhar Singh. Memory: 'Chak (wheel/chakkar) jaisi hoti hai Rai (round mustard seed) → Raigarh = Chakradhar Singh'. Note: Raigarh NOT Raipur.
Bharatanatyam (5 MCQs)
60
Which is the OLDEST classical dance form of India, over 2000 years old?
A. Kathak
B. Odissi
C. Bharatanatyam
D. Kuchipudi
Bharatanatyam (Tamil Nadu) is the oldest classical dance of India (2000+ years). Chronological order (oldest to newest): Bharatanatyam → Kathak → Manipuri → Mohini Attam (most recent among these).
61
Bharatanatyam originally originated from which tradition in Tamil Nadu temples?
A. Devadasi culture (Dasi Attam)
B. Warrior (Kshatriya) tradition
C. Royal court entertainment
D. Bhakti saint movement
Bharatanatyam originated from the Devadasi culture (girls dedicated to temples who danced for God). Originally called 'Dasi Attam'. Devadasi system was later exploited during colonization. Bharatanatyam represents the Fire element.
62
What is the CORRECT order of stages in a Bharatanatyam performance?
A. Tillana → Varnam → Alarippu
B. Alarippu → Jatiswaram → Shabdam → Varnam → Padam → Tillana
C. Varnam → Jatiswaram → Padam → Tillana
D. Padam → Shabdam → Alarippu → Tillana
Bharatanatyam performance stages: Alarippu → Jatiswaram → Shabdam → Varnam → Padam → Tillana. Starts with Alarippu and ENDS with Tillana (same as Carnatic music performance).
63
Who gave global recognition to Bharatanatyam and developed the Kalakshetra style?
A. E Krishna Iyer
B. T Bala Saraswati
C. Rukmini Devi Arundale
D. Yamini Krishnamurthy
Rukmini Devi Arundale developed the Kalakshetra style of Bharatanatyam and gave it global recognition. E Krishna Iyer is credited with reviving/saving the dance form. T Bala Saraswati = Queen of Bharatanatyam.
64
Who was the first transgender person to receive Padmashri for Bharatanatyam (2019)?
A. Sudha Chandran
B. Nartaki Natarajan
C. Alarmel Valli
D. Padma Subramaniam

Kathakali (4 MCQs)
65
Kathakali is known as 'Ballad of the East'. Which unique feature distinguishes it from other classical dances?
A. Female performers only
B. All-male troupe where males play female roles too
C. No facial makeup used
D. Performed only in temples
Kathakali (Kerala) is an all-male troupe — males play female characters too. Opposite is Manipuri, where all performers are female (females play male roles too). Called 'Ballad of the East' due to its epic storytelling.
66
Kathakali evolved from which two earlier dance forms?
A. Yakshagana and Theyyam
B. Ramanattam and Krishnanattam
C. Mohini Attam and Kuchipudi
D. Kuravanji and Therukoothu
Kathakali evolved from Ramanattam (dance on Ram's stories) and Krishnanattam (dance on Krishna's stories). 'Attam' means dance. Katha = story, Kali = art.
67
Who is credited with the revival of Kathakali?
A. Rukmini Devi Arundale
B. E Krishna Iyer
C. V N Menon
D. Guru Kunchukurup
V N Menon (with the patronage of king Mukunda Raja) is credited with reviving Kathakali. Note: The SAME V N Menon also revived Mohini Attam — both are Kerala dance forms.
68
Guru Kunchukurup (Kunchu Kurup) is known as the Maestro of which dance form?
A. Odissi
B. Manipuri
C. Mohini Attam
D. Kathakali
Guru Kunchukurup (Kunchu Kurup) is the Kathakali Maestro who popularized it worldwide. Memory: 'Kathakali mein roop (face paint) bahut important hota hai — Kunchu Kurup'. Mridupathi Sinha Nair, Gopi Nath, and Rita Ganguly are other important Kathakali exponents.
Mohini Attam (3 MCQs)
69
Mohini Attam is called the 'Dance of Mohini'. Who is Mohini in Hindu mythology?
A. Goddess Saraswati
B. Vishnu's female form who tricked demons
C. Goddess Parvati
D. A Gandharva apsara
Mohini Attam = Dance of Mohini (Vishnu's female form). During Samudra Manthan, Vishnu took the Mohini form to distract demons and give nectar to gods. Also linked to the Bhasmasura legend. The dance represents the Air element.
70
Which is the distinctive attire of Mohini Attam performers?
A. Red and gold
B. Off-white/white with golden brocade
C. Blue and silver
D. Green and orange
Mohini Attam performers wear off-white/white costume with golden brocade border — this is a fixed, distinctive attire unique to Mohini Attam. Only female dancers perform Mohini Attam (unlike Kathakali which is all-male).
71
V N Menon is credited with reviving which TWO Kerala classical dance forms?
A. Bharatanatyam and Kuchipudi
B. Kathakali and Mohini Attam
C. Odissi and Sattriya
D. Manipuri and Kathak
V N Menon revived BOTH Kathakali AND Mohini Attam — both are Kerala classical dances. This is an important connecting fact frequently asked in exams.

Kuchipudi gets its name from which village in Andhra Pradesh?
A. Kuchilapuri

Kuchipudi originated from the village of Kuchelapuram (also called Kushelavapuri) in Andhra Pradesh. It was performed on Bhagavat Purana stories. Flourished under Vijayanagara and Golkonda rulers. Represents the Earth element.

Raja and Radha Reddy are famous as the first husband-wife pair to receive which award both individually and simultaneously?
A. Padma Vibhushan

Raja and Radha Reddy (Kuchipudi dancers) are the FIRST husband-wife pair to individually AND simultaneously receive Padmashri and Padma Bhushan for contribution to Kuchipudi dance. Vempati Chinna Satyam revived Kuchipudi.
Odissi (4 MCQs)
74
Which are the TWO distinctive postures unique to Odissi dance?
A. Tandava and Lasya
B. Chauka and Tribhanga
C. Natya and Nritta
D. Alarippu and Tillana
Odissi's unique postures: Chauka (square posture — masculine/Shamanic stance) and Tribhanga (three-bend posture — feminine, body bent at neck, torso, and knees). Frequently asked in exams.
75
Odissi dance is traditionally performed on compositions from which classical text?
A. Ramcharitmanas
B. Geet Govind Ashtapadis by Jayadev
C. Meghadutam by Kalidasa
D. Mahabharata
Odissi is traditionally performed on Jayadev's Geet Govind Ashtapadis (8-line verses). Temple dancers (Maharis) and young boys (Gotipua) performed this dance. Evidence found in Udaygiri and Khandagiri caves. Odissi represents the Water element.
76
Who was the FIRST Odissi dancer to receive Padma Vibhushan?
A. Sonal Mansingh
B. Indrani Rahman
C. Kelucharan Mohapatra
D. Pankaj Charan Das
Kelucharan Mohapatra was the first Odissi dancer to receive Padma Vibhushan (2000). He is the most renowned Odissi exponent. Indrani Rahman (Miss India 1952) gave Odissi global recognition at White House.

Eliana Sitarist (2006, Padmashri) was notable for which distinction in Odissi?
A. First male Odissi dancer

D. First child prodigy in Odissi
Eliana Sitarist from Italy was the FIRST foreign-origin artist to receive Padmashri for contribution to Odissi dance (2006). Another key fact: Indrani Rahman (Miss India 1952) performed Odissi at the White House.
Manipuri (4 MCQs)
78
Manipuri dance traces its origins to which festival of Manipur?
A. Lai Haraoba
B. Yaoshang
C. Cheiraoba
D. Kanglen
Manipuri dance originated from the Lai Haraoba festival of Manipur. Originally it was Shiva-Parvati's dance. After the 15th century, Vaishnava culture was incorporated and it became centered around Krishna-Radha.
79
Which classical Indian dance uses the Pung (Manipuri classical drum) as its primary percussion instrument?
A. Bharatanatyam
B. Odissi
C. Sattriya
D. Manipuri
Manipuri dance uses the Pung (Manipuri classical drum). Unique feature: all performers are female — females play male roles too (opposite of Kathakali where all are male). Performers also wear a bamboo skirt and thin face veil.
80
Who is called the 'Father of modern Manipuri dance' (the Vaishnava form)?
A. Raja Bhagachandra
B. Rabindranath Tagore
C. Guru Bipin Singh
D. Jhaveri Sisters
Guru Bipin Singh established the modern form of Manipuri dance (Vaishnava culture-based). Memory: 'bahut saari money chahiye, pin kharidne ke liye → Bipin'. Rabindranath Tagore popularized it at Shanti Niketan.
81
Jhaveri Sisters were notable as the first non-Manipuri dancers to perform Manipuri Ras at?
A. Shanti Niketan, Bengal
B. Govind Devji Temple, Imphal
C. Nathdwara Temple, Rajasthan
D. Jagannath Temple, Puri
Jhaveri Sisters (4 sisters) were the first non-Manipuri dancers to perform Manipuri Ras at Govind Devji Temple, Imphal (Manipur). This is an -asked fact.
Sattriya (3 MCQs)
82
Sattriya, the classical dance of Assam, was founded by which saint in the 15th century?
A. Raja Bhagachandra
B. Sankardev Ray
C. Bishnu Prasad Rabha
D. Jyoti Prasad Agarwala
Sattriya was founded by Sankardev Ray in the 15th century in Vaishnava monasteries (Satras) of Assam. Memory: 'Sattriya = Satra (monastery) = Assam'. It is performed on Borgeet (devotional compositions by Sankardev). Connection: Kshatriya → Assam mnemonic = 'aasmaaN ki taraf dekh ke bhagwan ko yaad karo'.
83
Ankiya Naat is a dramatic style associated with which classical dance form?
A. Manipuri
B. Kathak
C. Sattriya
D. Kuchipudi
Ankiya Naat is a style of Sattriya where Krishna's stories are dramatized with colorful costumes — also called Bhavona. Other Sattriya-related terms: Borgeet (devotional songs), Mati Akhara (training ground), Goa Bhayanar Naach and Kharmananar Naach (two current forms).
84
Mati Akhara is a concept in which classical dance, serving as a physical training ground?
A. Odissi
B. Bharatanatyam
C. Manipuri
D. Sattriya
Mati Akhara (Mati Akhada = ground training) is a pre-performance physical training system in Sattriya — it develops flexibility, stamina, and physical strength before the actual dance performance.
Chhau Dance (4 MCQs)
85
The word 'Chhau' is derived from which Sanskrit word meaning shadow?
A. Chhaya
B. Chhavi
C. Chhand
D. Chhatra
Chhau is derived from 'Chhaya' (shadow). It has semi-classical nature with martial, tribal, and folk elements. Listed as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2010. Ministry of Culture considers it the 9th classical dance.
86
Which form of Chhau dance does NOT use a mask?
A. Seraikela Chhau (Jharkhand)
B. Purulia Chhau (West Bengal)
C. Mayurbhanj Chhau (Odisha)
D. All use masks
Mayurbhanj Chhau (Odisha) does NOT use a mask. Memory: 'Mayur (peacock) mask le ke ud gaya' = mask-less. Seraikela Chhau (Jharkhand) and Purulia Chhau (West Bengal) both use masks.
87
Purulia Chhau belongs to which state?
A. Odisha
B. Jharkhand
C. Bihar
D. West Bengal
Three Chhau types: Mayurbhanj Chhau → Odisha (no mask) | Seraikela Chhau → Jharkhand (mask) | Purulia Chhau → West Bengal (mask). Memory: 'Seraikela → Jharkhand: kelay (banana) jhadi mein ug rahi hai'.
88
In which year was Chhau dance inscribed in UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list?
A. 2003
B. 2008
C. 2010
D. 2016
Chhau dance was inscribed in UNESCO's Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2010. Other Indian entries on this list include Yoga, Kumbh Mela, Vedic chanting, Ramlila, etc.
Practice MCQs (PYQ Style) (11 MCQs)
89
Which musical form is described as having a 'sober atmosphere' and emphasizing rhythm strongly? (-type)
A. Khayal
B. Thumri
C. Tappa
D. Dhrupad
Dhrupad has a sober, structured nature with strong emphasis on notes and rhythm (taal). Khayal is expressive and improvisational. Thumri is romantic/semi-classical. Tappa is fast-paced folk. Answer: Dhrupad.
90
Which Gharana was directly influenced by the Dhrupad tradition in Hindustani music?
A. Gwalior Gharana
B. Jaipur-Atrauli Gharana
C. Rampur-Sahaswan Gharana
D. Agra Gharana
Agra Gharana is directly influenced by Dhrupad-Dhamar gayaki. Founded by Haji Sujan Khan (son-in-law of Tansen). Known for powerful, full-throated singing. Key artist: Faiyaz Khan.
91
Which of the following is NOT a raga-based performance?
A. Thumri
B. Dhrupad
C. Prabandh
D. Sonata
Sonata is a Western classical music composition form — it is NOT raga-based. Thumri (semi-classical), Dhrupad (classical), and Prabandh (ancient Sanskrit composition) are all raga-based. Prabandh predates even Dhrupad.
92
Arrange these classical dances from OLDEST to NEWEST: Bharatanatyam, Kathak, Manipuri, Mohini Attam
A. Mohini Attam → Kathak → Manipuri → Bharatanatyam
B. Bharatanatyam → Kathak → Manipuri → Mohini Attam
C. Kathak → Bharatanatyam → Mohini Attam → Manipuri
D. Manipuri → Kathak → Bharatanatyam → Mohini Attam
Chronological order: Bharatanatyam (oldest, 2000+ years) → Kathak (medieval period, Mughal era) → Manipuri (15th-17th century Vaishnava movement) → Mohini Attam (18th-20th century, most recent revival). Mohini Attam is the newest among these.

Lai Haraoba festival is associated with the origins of which classical dance?
A. Sattriya
B. Kathakali
C. Manipuri
D. Chhau
Lai Haraoba is a Manipur festival from which Manipuri dance traces its origins. It began as Shiva-Parvati's dance and later incorporated Vaishnava (Krishna-Radha) themes after the 15th century.

Vilasini Natyam, a classical dance style revived by Dr. Swapna Sundari, belongs to which state?

Vilasini Natyam is a classical dance style of Andhra Pradesh, originally performed by temple devadasis and later in royal courts. Revived in the 20th century by Dr. Swapna Sundari. Not counted among the standard 8 classical dances.
96
Mati Akhara is a physical training practice associated with which classical dance?
A. Odissi
B. Bharatanatyam

Mati Akhara (training ground) is part of Sattriya dance tradition from Assam — it trains performers in physical strength, flexibility, and stamina before they perform the actual dance.

Kalakshetra style of Bharatanatyam was developed by which dance guru?
A. T Bala Saraswati

Rukmini Devi Arundale developed the Kalakshetra style of Bharatanatyam and founded Kalakshetra Foundation in Chennai. She also gave Bharatanatyam global recognition. T Bala Saraswati = Queen of Bharatanatyam.
98
Who founded the Banaras Gharana of Kathak dance?
A. Birju Maharaj
B. Lachhu Maharaj
C. Janaki Prasad
D. Wajid Ali Shah
Banaras Gharana of Kathak: founded by Janaki Prasad. Memory: 'Banaras ka paan = prasad → Janaki Prasad'. The four Kathak Gharanas: Lucknow (Wajid Ali Shah), Jaipur (Bhanuji), Raigarh (Chakradhar Singh), Banaras (Janaki Prasad).
99
Guru Bipin Singh is associated with which classical dance form of India?
A. Odissi
B. Bharatanatyam
C. Sattriya
D. Manipuri
Guru Bipin Singh is the father of modern Manipuri dance (Vaishnava form centered on Krishna-Radha). Memory: 'Mani (money) chahiye, pin kharidne ke liye → Bipin → Manipuri'.
