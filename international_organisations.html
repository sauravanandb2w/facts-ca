How to remember the zoo of organisations

Think in jobs, not random names:
💰 Money crisis / surveillance → IMF
🏗️ Long-term development loans → World Bank Group
📦 Trade rules & disputes → WTO
🗣️ Davos talk-shop of elites → WEF
🏦 Regional/new lenders → ADB · AIIB · NDB
🇺🇳 World parliament + security → UN
🩺 Special jobs → WHO health, UNESCO education/culture, ILO labour…
🤝 Clubs of countries → G20, G7, BRICS, SCO, ASEAN, QUAD

Master Hindi scaffold

“पैसे IMF, निर्माण WB, व्यापार WTO, बातचीत WEF — बाकी क्लब और UN परिवार!”
IMF = firefighter for balance-of-payments · WB = builder with project loans · WTO = referee of trade · WEF = annual networking summit (not a treaty org like WTO).

Bretton Woods twin

“1944 ब्रेटन वुड्स — जुड़वाँ जन्मे: IMF बचाए, WB बनाए!”
Both HQ Washington DC. Same birth story, different jobs. Don’t mix SDRs (IMF) with IBRD project loans (WB).
🚑

IMF — International Monetary Fund

Bretton Woods twin · Crisis firefighter · Surveillance of economies
1944 · in force 1945 HQ: Washington DC India: founding member
  • Job: global monetary cooperation, exchange stability, temporary BOP financing, capacity development.
  • Resources: member quotas (subscription) — decide voting power + access to finance. Quotas reviewed periodically.
  • SDR (Special Drawing Rights): international reserve asset created by IMF; value from a basket of currencies (USD, EUR, CNY, JPY, GBP).
  • Surveillance: Article IV consultations — regular health-check of member economies.
  • Lending: Stand-By Arrangement, Extended Fund Facility, Rapid Financing Instrument, poverty-reduction tools, etc. Often with conditionality.
  • Leadership: Managing Director (conventionally European in older practice — know as a political convention, not a treaty rule carved in stone).
  • India angle: major emerging shareholder; uses IMF data/reports heavily in economy CA; not a “project builder” like WB.
IMF = “इमरजेंसी मनी फायरमैन” — कोटा = वोट + लोन की लिमिट; SDR = टोकरी वाली रिज़र्व मुद्रा इकाई।

Trade vs talk-shop

“WTO अदालत + नियम किताब है; WEF पहाड़ी मेला (Davos) है!”
WTO = member-driven treaty org with dispute settlement. WEF = Swiss foundation / forum — influential, but not a UN specialized agency.
⚖️

WTO — World Trade Organization

Global trade rule-book + dispute settlement
Born 1995 · GATT 1947 HQ: Geneva India: member
  • Predecessor: GATT 1947 (agreement, not a full org). Uruguay Round → Marrakesh Agreement → WTO 1995.
  • Principles: MFN, National Treatment, reciprocity, transparency, binding tariffs (know the names).
  • Pillars often tested: goods (GATT), services (GATS), IP (TRIPS), Dispute Settlement Understanding (DSU).
  • Ministerial Conference: top decision body; decisions usually by consensus.
  • India hot buttons: agriculture (food security/public stockholding), fisheries subsidies, e-commerce moratorium debates, special & differential treatment.
WTO = “व्यापार की अदालत जिनेवा में” — GATT पुरानी किताब, 1995 में संस्था बनी। MFN + National Treatment याद रखो।
🏔️

WEF — World Economic Forum

Public–private cooperation platform · Davos Annual Meeting
Founded 1971 Cologny, Switzerland Not a treaty org
  • Founded by Klaus Schwab.
  • Famous for Davos annual meeting — leaders from business, government, civil society.
  • Publishes reports (e.g. Global Competitiveness / risks style products — verify latest titles in CA).
  • Trap: WEF ≠ WTO ≠ World Bank. No binding trade dispute powers.
WEF = “पर्वत पर पिकनिक” (Davos) — बातें बहुत, कानून WTO जितने बाध्यकारी नहीं।
📉

UNCTAD

UN Conference on Trade and Development
1964GenevaUN body
  • Focus: trade–development link for developing countries; reports on investment, commodities, digital economy.
  • Often appears when CA discusses Global South trade/finance narratives.
UNCTAD = “विकासशील देशों की व्यापार-पुस्तिका” — जिनेवा, UN परिवार।

Three banks people mix up

“मनीला ADB, बीजिंग AIIB, शंघाई NDB — तीन बैंक, तीन शहर!”
ADB = older Asia-Pacific MDB · AIIB = China-led infra bank · NDB = BRICS bank.

ADB — Asian Development Bank

Asia-Pacific multilateral development bank
1966Manila, PhilippinesIndia: founding member

  • Japan & US are among the largest shareholders historically — classic Prelims fact family.
  • India is both shareholder and major borrower for state infra / urban / energy projects.
ADB = “एशिया का पुराना बैंक मनीला में”।

AIIB = “इन्फ्रा के लिए बीजिंग वाला नया बैंक” — India बड़ा शेयरधारक।

NDB — New Development Bank

BRICS bank
Fortaleza 2014 · HQ open ShanghaiShanghaiIndia: founding BRICS member

UN principal organs

“छह अंग याद रखो: असेम्बली, सुरक्षा, परिषद(ECOSOC), ट्रस्टीशिप(निष्क्रिय), कोर्ट, सचिवालय!”
UN Charter 1945, San Francisco. HQ New York. Trusteeship Council is inactive (no trust territories left).
🕊️

United Nations

Charter 1945 · HQ New York
24 Oct 1945New YorkIndia: founding member
  • UNGA: all members; one country one vote; recommendations (budget & some elections important).
  • UNSC: primary responsibility for peace & security; 5 permanent (US UK France Russia China) with veto + 10 non-permanent (2-year terms).
  • ECOSOC: economic & social coordination; links to specialized agencies.
  • ICJ: Hague — principal judicial organ; states are parties to cases.
  • Secretariat: Secretary-General + staff; SG is “chief administrative officer,” elected via UNSC recommendation + UNGA appointment.
  • India CA: UNSC reform demand (G4), peacekeeping contributions, elected UNSC terms in recent years.
UNSC = “पाँच वीटो राजा + दस मेहमान (2 साल)”। ICJ = हेग की अदालत।

Clubs vs organisations

“G20 मेज़ है, UN घर है — क्लब में सदस्यता, संधि-संगठन में क़ानून!”
G20/G7 are forums. ASEAN/SCO have charters/secretariats. NATO is a military alliance. Don’t call WEF a UN agency or G20 a treaty court.
🌐

G20

Premier forum for international economic cooperation
Finance 1999 · Leaders 2008+India Presidency 2023

  • No permanent secretariat like UN; presidency rotates; troika continuity.
  • India 2023: “Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam” theme / New Delhi Leaders’ Declaration — high-yield CA.
G20 = “दुनिया की आर्थिक मेज़” — प्रेसीडेंसी घूमती है, सचिवालय UN जैसा नहीं।

Practice MCQs

1. Bretton Woods twins with HQ in Washington DC are:
a) WTO and WEF
b) IMF and World Bank
c) ADB and AIIB
d) UNDP and UNEP
Answer: b. IMF + World Bank Group were created at Bretton Woods; both headquartered in Washington DC.
2. Which organisation is headquartered in Manila?
a) Asian Development Bank
b) AIIB
c) New Development Bank
d) WTO
Answer: a. ADB = Manila; AIIB = Beijing; NDB = Shanghai; WTO = Geneva.
3. Special Drawing Rights (SDRs) are associated with:
a) IMF
b) WTO Dispute Settlement Body
c) UNESCO
d) OPCW
Answer: a. SDRs are an IMF reserve asset.
4. WEF is best described as:
a) A UN specialized agency for trade disputes
b) A public–private forum known for the Davos meeting
c) A military alliance under Article 5
d) The secretariat of G20
Answer: b. WEF is not WTO, NATO, or the G20 secretariat.
5. Which pair is correctly matched?
a) UNESCO — Geneva
b) FAO — Paris
c) UNEP — Nairobi
d) ILO — Rome
Answer: c. UNESCO Paris, FAO Rome, ILO Geneva, UNEP Nairobi.
6. IDA is part of:
a) IMF
b) World Bank Group
c) WTO
d) AIIB only
Answer: b. IDA = soft-window arm of the World Bank Group.
7. RATS (anti-terror structure) is associated with:
a) NATO
b) SCO
c) QUAD
d) ASEAN
Answer: b. SCO’s RATS is in Tashkent.
8. OPCW implements which convention?
a) NPT
b) Chemical Weapons Convention
c) BTWC only (biological)
d) UNCLOS
Answer: b. OPCW = CWC implementing body, The Hague.
9. Which is NOT a principal organ of the UN?
a) ICJ
b) ECOSOC
c) WHO
d) Secretariat
Answer: c. WHO is a specialized agency, not one of the six principal organs.
10. New Development Bank HQ is in:
a) New Delhi
b) Beijing
c) Shanghai
d) Moscow
Answer: c. NDB — Shanghai.
