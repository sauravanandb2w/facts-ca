Q1
National Forest Martyrs Day commemorates which historical event?
(A) Chipko Movement, 1973
✅ (B) Khejarli Massacre, 1730
(C) Silent Valley Movement
(D) Appiko Movement
📖 Answer: (B)
National Forest Martyrs Day is observed on 11th September to commemorate the Khejarli Massacre of 1730 in Rajasthan, where over 300 Bishnoi community members, led by Amrita Devi Bishnoi, sacrificed their lives protecting Khejri (Prosopis cineraria) trees from being felled by Maharaja Abhay Singh's soldiers. Started by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change in 2013. The later Chipko Movement (1973, Chamoli, Uttarakhand) was inspired by the same spirit.
🧠 Mnemonic: Draw a simple "tree" shape with two strokes — it looks like "11". That's how 11th September is remembered for Forest Martyrs Day.
Q2
Hindi in the Devanagari script was adopted as an official language of the Union on which date, under which Constitutional Article?
(A) 26th January 1950, Article 348
✅ (B) 14th September 1949, Article 343(1)
(C) 2nd October 1950, Article 350
(D) 15th August 1947, Article 345
📖 Answer: (B)
Hindi Diwas is observed on 14th September to mark the adoption of Hindi in Devanagari script as an official language on 14th September 1949, under Article 343(1). Article 343(2) gave a 15-year transition period (1950-1965) for continuing English. The Official Languages Act, 1963 allowed English to continue beyond 1965 due to protests in South India. India has no National Language — only two Official Languages (Hindi and English) and 22 Scheduled Languages (8th Schedule). World Hindi Day is separately observed on 10th January.
Q3
What is the 2026 theme of the International Day for Preservation of the Ozone Layer, observed on 16th September?
(A) Montreal Protocol at 35
✅ (B) Global Action for a Cooler Planet
(C) Healing the Ozone Layer
(D) Ozone for Life
📖 Answer: (B)
The 2026 theme is "Global Action for a Cooler Planet." The day marks the signing of the Montreal Protocol on Substances that Deplete the Ozone Layer (16th September 1987, Montreal, Canada), which reduced use of CFCs, halons and carbon tetrachloride. The Kigali Amendment (2016, Rwanda) later added HFCs — which don't deplete ozone but are powerful greenhouse gases. UNGA proclaimed World Ozone Day in 1994.
🧠 Mnemonic: Imagine your "sweet 16th" birthday wish is that the ozone layer stays intact so UV rays don't damage your skin — that links 16th September to Ozone Day. Use Teachers' Day (5th) as your anchor point for the whole month's dates.
