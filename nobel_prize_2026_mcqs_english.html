2026 Laureates

🧠
Physiology or Medicine · 5 Oct

Karl Deisseroth · Peter Hegemann · Georg Nagel

For: discoveries on light-gated ion channels and optogenetics (controlling neurons with light).
Deisseroth – USA (Stanford) · Hegemann – Germany (Humboldt Univ.) · Nagel – Germany (Würzburg)
Awarded by: Karolinska Institute
🧠 "LIGHT" → medicine mein light jalao, neuron chalao!Dekho Hari Naach = Deisseroth–Hegemann–Nagel
🧊
Physics · 6 Oct

Francis Halzen (solo)

For: decisive contributions to IceCube Neutrino Observatory & discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin.
Belgian-born American · b. 1944, Tienen · Univ. of Wisconsin–Madison
Awarded by: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences
🧠 "ICE" → Halzen = "Hall of Ice": Antarctica ki barf mein bhooton (ghost particles) ka shikaar!IceCube = South Pole, ~1 km³ barf
🪞
Chemistry · 7 Oct

Henri B. Kagan · Kenso Soai

For: discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis (mirror-image molecules, origin of homochirality).
Kagan – France (b. 1930; non-linear effects, 1986) · Soai – Japan (b. 1950; Soai reaction/autocatalysis)
Awarded by: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences
🧠 "MIRROR" → Seedha haath vs ulta haath (chiral). Kagan = Kaun sa haath? Soai = So-ya (autocatalysis: khud ko banata hai).Kagan (Fr) + Soai (Jp) = KS
📜
Literature · 8 Oct

Anne Carson

For: "bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms for contemporary literature."
Canadian poet, classicist, translator · b. 21 Jun 1950, Toronto
Books: Autobiography of Red (1998), Nox (2010), If Not, Winter (Sappho), Norma Jeane Baker of Troy
Awarded by: Swedish Academy
🧠 "POEM" → Carson ki car mein Greek (classical) sawari, naye form ki kavita!Red = Autobiography of Red
⚖️
Peace · 9 Oct

Navi Pillay

For: efforts to promote peace and international law; ensuring war crimes, crimes against humanity and genocide are prosecuted.
South African jurist · b. 23 Sep 1941, Durban · ex-UN High Commissioner for Human Rights, ICC judge
Awarded by: Norwegian Nobel Committee (Oslo)
🧠 "JUSTICE" → Pillay = Pillar of Law: kanoon ka khamba, Africa se!
📈
Economic Sciences · 12 Oct

Robert E. Hall · Michael Woodford

For: microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation (models to manage economic shocks).
Both USA · Hall – Stanford (b. 1943) · Woodford – Columbia (b. 1955)
Awarded by: Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences (Riksbank Prize in memory of Nobel)
🧠 "SHOCK" → Hall mein Wood (lakdi) ka sahara: economy ke jhatke sambhalo!Stanford (Hall) / Columbia (Woodford)

Static GK – Nobel Prize

TopicFact
FounderAlfred Nobel (Swedish chemist, inventor of dynamite); will signed 1895, died 10 Dec 1896
First awarded1901 (5 categories). Economics added 1969 (Sveriges Riksbank prize, not an original Nobel)
Categories (6)Physics, Chemistry, Physiology/Medicine, Literature, Peace, Economic Sciences
Physics · Chemistry · EconomicsRoyal Swedish Academy of Sciences
MedicineKarolinska Institute (Nobel Assembly)
LiteratureSwedish Academy
PeaceNorwegian Nobel Committee – only prize awarded in Oslo (Norway); others in Stockholm
Ceremony date10 December (Nobel's death anniversary)
AnnouncementsFirst full week of October (Medicine on Monday)
RulesMax 3 laureates per prize; not awarded posthumously (except if death after announcement); Peace can go to organisations
AwardGold medal, diploma, cash prize

Key Terms from 2026

TermMeaning
OptogeneticsUsing light-sensitive proteins (channelrhodopsin) to switch neurons on/off
Ion channelProtein pore in cell membrane letting ions pass
NeutrinoNear-massless neutral "ghost" particle; passes through matter easily
IceCubeNeutrino detector buried in Antarctic ice at South Pole (~1 km³); first high-energy astrophysical neutrinos seen 2013
Chirality / HomochiralityMirror-image molecules (left/right handed); life uses only one hand
Asymmetric synthesisMaking one mirror-image form selectively (used in drugs)
AutocatalysisProduct of a reaction speeds up its own formation (Soai reaction)
Macro stabilisationPolicy to smooth booms/recessions and shocks

Mnemonics (Hinglish)

🧠 1 — 6 Categories

Physics Chemistry Medicine Literature Peace Economics = "PC ML PE"
📖 "PC (computer) par ML (machine learning) seekho, PE (physical education) bhi karo!" Economics sabse baad mein (1969) aaya.

🧠 2 — Kaun Award Deta Hai?

"PCE = Royal Academy · M = Karolinska · L = Swedish Academy · P = Norway"
Physics–Chemistry–Economics → Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences
📖 "Teen science wale (P-C-E) ek hi Royal Academy mein padhte hain. Medicine ka Doctor Karolinska gaya. Likhne wale Swedish Academy mein. Shanti ke liye Norway ki Peace-Committee."

🧠 3 — 2026 Winners: "L-I-M-P-J-S"

Light · Ice · Mirror · Poem · Justice · Shock
Medicine = Light (optogenetics) · Physics = Ice (IceCube) · Chemistry = Mirror molecules · Literature = Poem (Carson) · Peace = Justice (Pillay) · Economics = Shock (stabilisation)
📖 "Ek raat Antarctica ki Ice par Light jalayi, Mirror mein dekha, ek Poem likhi, Justice maanga, aur Economy ke Shock sambhale."

🧠 4 — Medicine Trio

"Dekho Hari Naach" = Deisseroth, Hegemann, Nagel
📖 "Hari ne light on ki, neuron naach utha!" (Deisseroth USA; Hegemann & Nagel Germany)

🧠 5 — Countries

Physics = 1 USA/Belgian · Chemistry = France + Japan · Lit = Canada · Peace = S. Africa · Econ = 2 USA
📖 "Kagan-Soai = France-Japan: 'Fr-Jp' = Free-Jump (mirror ke andar kood gaye)."

🧠 6 — Nobel Basics

"1895 will · 1896 death · 1901 first · 1969 Economics"
📖 "Nobel ne 95 mein wasiyat likhi, 96 mein chale gaye, 1901 mein pehla inaam, aur Economics 69 mein 'sixty-nine' ki tarah ulta-seedha aaya."
