Why "UN" at all?

1945 = युद्ध के बाद — शांति, सहयोग, कानून; League of Nations की जगह मजबूत संस्था
League of Nations (1919) failed to stop WWII. San Francisco Conference adopted the UN Charter. USSR & USA both founding members. India = founding member.

Charter anchors (static)

  • Purposes (Art. 1): maintain peace & security; friendly relations; international cooperation; harmonize actions.
  • Principles (Art. 2): sovereign equality; fulfil obligations in good faith; peaceful settlement; no threat of force; assist UN; non-intervention in domestic jurisdiction (with SC exception).
  • Commenced: 24 October 1945 → UN Day.
  • HQ: New York (land = international territory style arrangement).
Common mistake: Treating every global meeting as "the UN". A COP is a meeting of parties to a treaty (e.g. UNFCCC). UNEP is a UN programme. WHO is a specialized agency. All are in the "UN system" loosely, but legally different.

Six principal organs — exam must-know

GA · SC · ECOSOC · Trusteeship(मृत) · ICJ · Secretariat — छह, सात नहीं!

1. General Assembly (UNGA)

All member states · one country, one vote
  • Deliberative, policy-making, representative body.
  • Discusses any matter within Charter scope; recommends (binding mainly on budget, scales of assessment, some elections).
  • Elects non-permanent UNSC members, ECOSOC members, judges (with SC), approves UN budget.

2. Security Council (UNSC)

Primary responsibility: international peace & security
  • 5 permanent (P5): USA, UK, France, Russia, China — veto on substantive resolutions.
  • 10 non-permanent — 2-year terms; regional rotation.
  • Can authorize sanctions, peacekeeping, use of force (Chapter VII).
  • India: largest troop contributor historically; demands permanent seat (G4 narrative).

3. ECOSOC

Economic and Social Council — coordination hub
  • Coordinates economic, social, environmental work; links UN to specialized agencies & NGOs.
  • Regional commissions (e.g. UNECE, ESCAP) sit under ECOSOC.
  • Not the same as running climate law — that's UNFCCC treaty (separate page later).

4. Trusteeship Council

Inactive — trick question favourite
  • Oversaw trust territories; last territory (Palau) ended trusteeship.
  • Council suspended operations — still listed as principal organ in Charter.

5. ICJ

International Court of Justice · The Hague
  • Principal judicial organ; disputes between states that consent.
  • Advisory opinions to UN organs/agencies (e.g. GA, SC).
  • 15 judges; 9-year terms; separate from ICC (criminal individuals).

6. Secretariat

International civil service
  • Secretary-General — chief administrative officer; not world government.
  • Appointed: UNSC recommendation (often P5 consensus politics) + UNGA appointment.
  • Staff implement GA/SC decisions; SG can bring matters to SC attention (Art. 99).

"How many groups in the UN?" — four meanings

क्षेत्रीय समूह · G77 जैसे ब्लॉक · UN परिवार · उपसमितियाँ — एक शब्द, चार मतलब!

A. Five UN regional groups (for elections)

Not separate organizations — caucuses for rotating seats (UNSC non-permanent, ECOSOC, etc.). India = Asia-Pacific Group.

GroupTypical role

B. Political blocs at the UN (not Charter organs)

  • G77 + China — developing countries' coalition in UN economic negotiations (not 77 members today).
  • NAM — Non-Aligned Movement; Cold War legacy; consultative, not a UN body.
  • G4 — India, Brazil, Germany, Japan — mutual support for UNSC permanent seats.
  • P5 — permanent UNSC members with veto (not a "group" in Charter, but exam shorthand).
  • EU — coordinates voting at UN on many issues (27 states).

C. Subsidiary & functional bodies (examples)

Dozens exist — learn categories, not every name.

  • Under UNSC: sanctions committees, peacekeeping mandates.
  • Under GA: Sixth Committee (legal), Fifth Committee (budget), human rights bodies.
  • Under ECOSOC: functional commissions (statistics, population, etc.).

Linked to "UN" in news — but different legal animal

COP = संधि की बैठक · IAEA = परमाणु · WTO = व्यापार · ICC = अपराध अदालत

Multilateral Environmental Agreements (MEAs)

UNFCCC, CBD, CITES, Montreal Protocol, Ramsar…

States are Parties. Supreme body often called COP (Conference of the Parties). Secretariats may be hosted in UN system (e.g. climate secretariat linked to UNEP history) but the treaty is boss. Full ladder → UN Environment Architecture.

IAEA

International Atomic Energy Agency — Vienna

Autonomous; reports to UNGA & UNSC. Safeguards, nuclear verification. Not one of 15 specialized agencies in the classic list sense — "related organization".

WTO

World Trade Organization — Geneva

Trade rules; successor to GATT 1947. Cooperates with UN system but not a UN specialized agency. Details → IO trade tab.

ICC

International Criminal Court — The Hague

Rome Statute 1998 — tries individuals for genocide, crimes against humanity, war crimes. Not a UN organ (often confused with ICJ).

IPCC

Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change

Scientific assessments for policymakers; supports UNFCCC negotiations. Does not set law or run COP.

Peacekeeping

  • Authorized by UNSC; troops/police contributed by member states — no standing UN army.
  • Blue helmets; principles: consent of parties, impartiality, minimum use of force except self-defence / mandate.
  • India: among largest contributors — favourite CA/static pairing with UNSC reform.

Budget & assessment

  • Regular budget — assessed contributions; Fifth Committee (GA) deals with budget.
  • Peacekeeping — separate assessment scale.
  • P5 pay larger share on regular budget (with discounts/ceilings — know concept, not every %).

Secretary-General (quick)

  • 5-year term, renewable; practice of regional rotation.
  • Art. 99: may bring to SC attention any matter threatening peace.
