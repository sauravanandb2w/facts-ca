📝 Practice MCQs

Q1
2026 Australian Open Men's Singles winner?
A. Novak Djokovic
B. Carlos Alcaraz
C. Alexander Zverev
D. Jannik Sinner
CASA trick: C = Carlos Alcaraz → Australian Open (1st Grand Slam of year). Memory: Current Account → C → Carlos.
Q2
Which player won TWO Men's Singles Grand Slams in 2026?
A. Carlos Alcaraz
B. Jannik Sinner
C. Alexander Zverev
D. Rafael Nadal
CASA: A appears twice (French Open + US Open) = Alexander Zverev won both.
Q3
2026 Wimbledon Men's Singles winner?
A. Carlos Alcaraz
B. Alexander Zverev
C. Jannik Sinner
D. Holger Rune
CASA: S = Sinner → Wimbledon (3rd Grand Slam). Memory: Savings account → S → Sinner.
Q4
2026 Women's Singles – who won Australian Open AND US Open?
A. Mirra Andreeva
B. Linda Nosková
C. Aryna Sabalenka
D. Elena Rybakina
EMNE: E appears at Entry (Australian) and Exit (US Open) → Elena Rybakina won both!
Q5
2026 French Open Women's Singles winner?
A. Elena Rybakina
B. Coco Gauff
C. Mirra Andreeva
D. Iga Swiatek
EMNE: M = Mirra Andreeva → French Open (2nd Grand Slam). Remember M is in the middle of the court.
Q6
Which Grand Slam is played on CLAY court?
A. Australian Open
B. French Open
C. Wimbledon
D. US Open
Courts: Australian=Hard, French=Clay (Roland Garros), Wimbledon=Grass, US Open=Hard. Only French Open uses clay.
Q7
Wimbledon is the world's oldest Grand Slam. When did it start?
A. 1877
B. 1881
C. 1891
D. 1905
Wimbledon started in 1877 (grass court). US Open=1881, French Open=1891, Australian Open=1905. Memory: Wimbledon is Oldest → earliest year.
Q8
Most Men's Singles Grand Slam titles (all-time)?
A. Roger Federer – 20
B. Rafael Nadal – 22
C. Novak Djokovic – 24
D. Pete Sampras – 14
Novak Djokovic holds the all-time record with 24 Grand Slam Men's Singles titles. Nadal=22, Federer=20.
Q9
Rafael Nadal holds the record for most titles at which Grand Slam?
A. Australian Open
B. French Open
C. Wimbledon
D. US Open
Nadal won French Open 14 times — nicknamed 'King of Clay'. It is the all-time record for a single Grand Slam.
Q10
Rohan Bopanna became the oldest man to win a Grand Slam title. Which title was it?
A. 2024 Wimbledon Mixed Doubles
B. 2024 Australian Open Men's Doubles
C. 2023 US Open Men's Doubles
D. 2024 French Open Mixed Doubles
Rohan Bopanna won 2024 Australian Open Men's Doubles (with Matthew Ebden) at age 43 years 329 days — oldest man to win a Grand Slam title.
Q11
Leander Paes was inducted into International Tennis Hall of Fame in which year?
A. 2020
B. 2022
C. 2023
D. 2024
Leander Paes was inducted into the International Tennis Hall of Fame in 2024. He also won Bronze at 1996 Atlanta Olympics in Men's Singles.
Q12
Which statement about US Open is WRONG?
A. Started in 1881 as US National Championship
B. Played on Grass Court
C. Played in August–September
D. Held at Flushing Meadows, New York
US Open is played on HARD court (not grass). It was grass earlier, then clay, but now hard court. French Open=Clay, Wimbledon=Grass.
