📋 All Five Year Plans — At a Glance
1st Five Year Plan1951–56
🌾 Agriculture + Irrigation + Power
Harrod-Domar Model
Target: 2.1%
Achieved: 3.6% ✅
Key events: Bhakra-Nangal Dam, Community Dev Programme (1952), National Extension Service
Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru
🧠 1 = "ANN" → First need = FOOD → Agriculture first → Like a baby, India's first need was FOOD!
2nd Five Year Plan1956–61
🏭 Heavy Industries (Steel, Power)
Nehru-Mahalanobis Model (PC Mahalanobis)
Target: 4.5%
Achieved: 4.27% ✅
Steel Plants: Bhilai (Russia), Rourkela (Germany), Durgapur (UK)
Key: Industrial Policy Resolution 1956, Atomic Energy Act
Chairman: Nehru
🧠 2 = "BHARI" (Heavy) → Mahalanobis = Maha + Loh (Metal) → BIG METAL PLAN! → 3 steel plants in ONE plan!
3rd Five Year Plan1961–66
💪 Self-Reliance + Agriculture
John Sandee / Gadgil approach
Target: 5.6%
Achieved: 2.84% ❌ FAILED
Why failed? China war (1962) + Pakistan war (1965) + Droughts
Chairman: Nehru (died 1964) → Lal Bahadur Shastri
🧠 3 = "TEEN DUSHMAN" → THREE problems: China + Pakistan + Drought = Plan FAILED = "Selfie bhi kharaab aaya!"
⛔ Plan Holiday (1966–69) — 3 Annual Plans. Reason: Wars (1962, 1965) + Drought + Rupee devalued (1966). Green Revolution STARTED during this period. 🧠 Trick: "Teen saal chhutti kyun? Teen problems ke baad!"
4th Five Year Plan1969–74
📈 Growth with Stability + Self-Reliance
Ashok Rudra + Alan Manne model
Target: 5.7%
Achieved: 3.3% ❌
Key: 14 Banks nationalized (1969), Green Revolution results, Garibi Hatao slogan (1971), IADP
Chairman: Indira Gandhi
🧠 4 = "CHAR BANK" → 14 banks nationalized (CHAR+10!) → Indira = India ki BANK wali PM → 1969 mein banks ka CHAR baja diya!
5th Five Year Plan1974–79
🤝 Poverty Removal + Self-Reliance
DP Dhar model
Target: 4.4%
Achieved: 4.8% ✅
Key: 20-point Programme (Indira), Minimum Needs Programme (MNP), SFDA
Terminated early (1978) by Janata govt (was to end 1979)
Chairman: Indira Gandhi
🧠 5 = "PAANCH SAAL" → But only 4 saal chala! → Janata govt ne 1 saal pehle terminate kiya! → "Paanch wada, chaar diya" = Poverty pe focus, kaam adhura!
🔄 Rolling Plan (1978–80) — Janata Party govt (Morarji Desai). Designed by DT Lakdawala. Concept: Revise targets every year. 🧠 Trick: "Janata ROLLING karte rahe, kuch set nahi kiya!"

📝 Practice MCQs — Five Year Plans (15 Questions)

Q1. Which model was used in India's 1st Five Year Plan?
A. Mahalanobis Model
B. Harrod-Domar Model
C. John Sandee Model
D. DP Dhar Model
Explanation: The Harrod-Domar model was used in the 1st FYP (1951–56) which focused on Agriculture. The Mahalanobis model came in the 2nd FYP (Heavy Industries). 🧠 Trick: 1st plan = 1st things = Food = Agriculture = H-D (Harrod-Domar) like "Hard work for food!"
Q2. Bhilai Steel Plant was set up with assistance of which country?
A. UK
B. Germany
C. USA
D. Soviet Union (USSR)
Explanation: Bhilai → USSR (Russia), Rourkela → Germany (West), Durgapur → UK. 🧠 Trick: BRD = Bhilai-Russia-Durgapur; or "Bharat Russia Dekh" = Bhilai with Russia!
Q3. The 3rd Five Year Plan is also called "Gadgil Yojana". Why did it FAIL?
A. Only drought
B. China war only
C. China war + Pakistan war + Droughts
D. Oil crisis
Explanation: 3rd FYP failed due to THREE reasons: (1) India-China War 1962, (2) India-Pakistan War 1965, (3) Severe droughts. Achieved only 2.84% against 5.6% target. 🧠 Trick: 3rd plan = 3 TEEN DUSHMAN = 3 enemies (China + Pak + Drought)
Q4. How many banks were nationalized during the 4th Five Year Plan period?
A. 8 banks
B. 12 banks
C. 14 banks
D. 20 banks
Explanation: In 1969 (4th FYP period), 14 major commercial banks were nationalized under Indira Gandhi. 🧠 Trick: "CHAR(4) + DAS(10) = 14 banks nationalized!"
Q5. Which Five Year Plan was terminated one year ahead of schedule?
A. 3rd Five Year Plan
B. 4th Five Year Plan
C. 5th Five Year Plan
D. 6th Five Year Plan
Explanation: The 5th FYP (1974–79) was terminated in 1978 (1 year early) by the Janata Party government under Morarji Desai. 🧠 Trick: "5th plan ka 5th year cancel by Janata = Paanch wada, chaar diya!"
Q6. The Rolling Plan (1978–80) was associated with which government?
A. Indira Gandhi govt
B. Janata Party (Morarji Desai)
C. Rajiv Gandhi govt
D. VP Singh govt
Explanation: The Rolling Plan was introduced by the Janata Party (Morarji Desai). It was designed by D.T. Lakdawala. The idea: revise plan targets annually. 🧠 Trick: "Janata ROLLING karte rahe, koi fixed plan nahi!"
Q7. IRDP (Integrated Rural Development Programme) was launched during which plan?
A. 5th FYP
B. 6th FYP
C. 7th FYP
D. 8th FYP
Explanation: IRDP was a flagship scheme of the 6th FYP (1980–85). Also launched: NREP (National Rural Employment Programme) and TRYSEM. 🧠 Trick: "6 = CHHA schemes for garib = IRDP = I Reduce Daridrya Programme!"
Q8. Which Five Year Plan focused on "Human Development" for the first time?
A. 7th FYP
B. 8th FYP
C. 9th FYP
D. 10th FYP
Explanation: The 8th FYP (1992–97) was the FIRST plan to focus on Human Development. This also coincided with the LPG Reforms (Narasimha Rao + Manmohan Singh). 🧠 Trick: "8 = AATH dimensions of HUMAN Development = 1st HUMAN plan!"
Q9. Which plan achieved the HIGHEST growth rate among all Five Year Plans?
A. 10th FYP (7.6%)
B. 11th FYP (8.0%)
C. 12th FYP (6.7%)
D. 8th FYP (6.8%)
Explanation: The 11th FYP (2007–12) achieved the highest growth rate of 8% (target was 9%). This was under PM Manmohan Singh. RTE 2009 and Aadhaar 2009 were launched during this period. 🧠 Trick: "GYARAH (11) = Sabse GYAADA (highest) growth = 8% = Inclusive ka best result!"
Q10. NITI Aayog replaced Planning Commission on which date?
A. January 1, 2014
B. January 1, 2015
C. August 15, 2014
D. March 1, 2015
Explanation: Planning Commission was dissolved and NITI Aayog was formed on January 1, 2015. Full form: National Institution for Transforming India. PM Modi is its ex-officio chairman. 🧠 Trick: "New Year 2015 = New India = NITI Aayog = Planning Commission GONE on New Year's Day!"
Q11. What was the focus of India's 7th Five Year Plan?
A. Poverty removal and self-reliance
B. Food, Work, and Productivity
C. Human development
D. Heavy industries
Explanation: 7th FYP (1985–90) under Rajiv Gandhi focused on Food, Work, and Productivity. JRY (Jawahar Rozgar Yojana) and Technology Mission were key. 🧠 Trick: "7 = SAAT = Rajiv Gandhi's 3 pillars: KHANA (food), KAAM (work), KAMAAL (productivity)!"
Q12. The 12th Five Year Plan's theme "Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth" added which NEW word compared to 11th plan?
A. Faster
B. Inclusive
C. Sustainable
D. More
Explanation: 11th plan = "Faster and More Inclusive Growth". 12th plan added "Sustainable" → "Faster, More Inclusive and Sustainable Growth". This was the last FYP. 🧠 Trick: "12th = LAST plan adds SUSTAINABILITY = Earth bhi bachao, plan bhi khatam!"
Q13. During the 'Plan Holiday' (1966–69), which revolutionary change in agriculture took place?
A. White Revolution
B. Blue Revolution
C. Green Revolution
D. Yellow Revolution
Explanation: The Green Revolution began during the Plan Holiday (1966–69) with HYV (High Yielding Variety) seeds introduced by MS Swaminathan + Norman Borlaug. Punjab, Haryana, UP benefited most. 🧠 Trick: "Plan Holiday = CHHUTTI = Green grass grows during holiday = Green Revolution!"
Q14. Which Five Year Plan introduced the concept of "Monitorable Targets" for the first time?
A. 9th FYP
B. 10th FYP
C. 11th FYP
D. 8th FYP
Explanation: The 10th FYP (2002–07) introduced "Monitorable Targets" for the first time — specific measurable goals like halving poverty ratio, reducing IMR, increasing literacy. 🧠 Trick: "10 = DAS targets monitor karo = 10th plan measures EVERYTHING with numbers!"
Q15. NITI Aayog's Governing Council includes which of the following?
A. All Cabinet Ministers only
B. All Chief Ministers + Lt. Governors of UTs
C. Finance Commission members
D. RBI Governor + Chief Ministers
Explanation: NITI Aayog's Governing Council = All Chief Ministers of states + Lt. Governors of UTs (with legislature). PM is Chairman. This embodies cooperative federalism. 🧠 Trick: "NITI = All CMs in ONE room = States have VOICE now = Cooperative Federalism!"
