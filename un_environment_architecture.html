Landmark UN environment conferences (political spine)

1972 Stockholm — UN Conference on the Human Environment. Theme: development vs environment. Created UNEP; “Only One Earth”; catalysed national env ministries & laws.
1987 Brundtland Report — “Our Common Future”; sustainable development vocabulary (not a COP).
1992 Rio Earth Summit (UNCED) — Rio de Janeiro. Rio trio signed/opened: UNFCCC, CBD, UNCCD framework. Also Agenda 21, Rio Declaration (27 principles — CBDR, precautionary idea).
2002 Johannesburg (Rio+10) — World Summit on Sustainable Development; implementation focus.
2012 Rio+20 — renewed political commitment; SDGs process accelerated.
2015 Paris — Paris Agreement under UNFCCC (COP21), not a new “UN organ”.

Exam sorting question

  • Conference = diplomatic moment (Stockholm, Rio).
  • Convention / treaty = ongoing legal regime (UNFCCC, CBD).
  • Protocol = treaty under a convention (Kyoto under UNFCCC; Montreal under Vienna).
  • COP = annual (or periodic) meeting of Parties to that convention.

What UNEP does

  • Sets global environmental agenda within the UN system.
  • Coordinates reporting, science-policy interfaces (with partners).
  • Hosts or supports secretariats (e.g. ozone secretariat historically; many MEAs “in the UN family”).
  • Does not replace UNFCCC — climate law lives in the climate convention.

Ozone ladder

  • Vienna Convention 1985 — framework for ozone layer protection
  • Montreal Protocol 1987 — binding phase-out of ODS (CFCs etc.)
  • Kigali Amendment 2016 — HFC phasedown (climate co-benefit)
  • Secretariat: Nairobi (UNEP ozone secretariat)
